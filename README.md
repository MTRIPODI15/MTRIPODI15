What I do

- **Requirements & acceptance criteria:** gather needs with business areas and turn them into scenarios and criteria the team can build and validate.
- **UAT & stakeholder bridge:** coordinate user acceptance with end users; technical counterpart to vendors.
- **Risk and impact analysis:** assess each change by its financial, reputational and operational impact.
- **Interbank certification:** COELSA, Red Link and Interbanking, before services go to production.
- **Testing:** functional, regression, E2E, smoke and sanity; API testing and automation as support.
- **Business data:** SQL and operational metrics; I also ran my own business and made decisions with its data.
- **Banking domain:** transfers | interbank clearing | credit & debit cards | e-checks | QR payments | loans | foreign trade | online banking | core banking
